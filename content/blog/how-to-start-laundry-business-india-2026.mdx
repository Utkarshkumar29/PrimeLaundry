# How to Start a Laundry Business in India: The Complete 2026 Guide

India's organised laundry market is worth ₹5,000 crore today — and still only **2% of the total laundry market**. The remaining 98% is unorganised: local dhobis, hand-washing, and corner ironing stalls.

That gap is your opportunity.

## Why the Laundry Business Is One of India's Best Opportunities Right Now

### 1. Daily recurring demand
Every household generates laundry every single day. Unlike food delivery (which competes on price and attention) or retail (which fights trends), laundry is **non-negotiable, non-seasonal, and non-discretionary**.

### 2. Rising disposable income + time poverty
Urban Indians are working longer hours. Nuclear families can't manage household chores. Even tier-2 cities like Raipur, Indore, and Nagpur are seeing rapid change in lifestyle.

### 3. Low penetration = massive headroom
The top 5 organised laundry brands in India collectively serve less than 1 crore customers. India has 35 crore households. The math speaks for itself.

## What Is the FOCO Model?

**FOCO = Franchise Owned, Company Operated.**

You invest in the physical infrastructure — the space, the equipment. The brand (Prime Laundry) operates the business: hiring staff, managing operations, sourcing orders, running marketing, and maintaining quality.

You earn a profit share. You don't need to run daily operations.

This is fundamentally different from a traditional franchise where you hire and manage staff yourself.
