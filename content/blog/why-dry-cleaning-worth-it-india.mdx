# Why Dry Cleaning Is Worth It: The Complete Guide for Indian Households

If you've ever ruined a silk saree in the washing machine or sent a suit to the wrong laundry and got it back shrunk — this guide is for you.

Dry cleaning isn't a luxury. For certain fabrics, it's the **only safe option**.

---

## What Is Dry Cleaning, Actually?

Contrary to the name, dry cleaning does use liquid — just not water. It uses a chemical solvent (typically perchloroethylene or, in modern eco-friendly services, hydrocarbon-based solvents) that dissolves oils, grease, and stains without wetting the fabric fibers.

This matters because water causes:
- Shrinkage in wool and silk
- Color bleeding in synthetic blends
- Warping in structured garments like blazers
- Water marks on delicate embroidery

---

## Which Fabrics Need Dry Cleaning?

| Fabric | Why Dry Cleaning |
|--------|-----------------|
| Pure silk | Water destroys fiber structure |
| Wool & cashmere | Felts and shrinks when wet |
| Heavy embroidery (zardozi, etc.) | Water loosens thread work |
| Structured suits & blazers | Padding and lining warp |
| Velvet | Water crushes pile permanently |
| Leather & suede trim | Water stains irreversibly |

**Rule of thumb:** If your garment has the symbol of a circle inside a square on the care label — it's dry clean only.

---

## The 5 Signs Your Clothes Need Professional Dry Cleaning

1. **The care label says "Dry Clean Only"** — Never ignore this. Brands put this because they tested it. Machine washing will void any guarantee.

2. **It's embroidered, beaded, or sequined** — Water can dissolve glue holding decorative elements. One wash can ruin ₹15,000 worth of work.

3. **It's a structured garment** — Suits, sherwanis, blazers, and formal kurtas have internal canvas or padding that shifts in water.

4. **It smells but isn't visibly dirty** — Dry cleaning removes odour molecules that water-based washing leaves behind.

5. **It has an oil-based stain** — Cooking oil, ghee, grease — these bind to fabric differently and require solvent, not water.

## Common Dry Cleaning Myths — Debunked

**"Dry cleaning damages clothes over time"**
Only if done excessively. For seasonal garments (wedding wear, winter coats), once or twice a year is ideal.

**"I can use dry cleaning spray at home"**
Home dry cleaning kits are surface fresheners. They don't remove stains or sanitize fabric.

**"All laundries do the same thing"**
No. Most small laundries use water for "dry cleaning" because actual equipment is expensive. Always ask about their process.
